# Pickle Advisors Organic Editorial Revision Prompt

## Source style

Use **Organic Editorial** as the primary structural reference. Translate its warmth, storytelling, asymmetry, real imagery, and mixed editorial rhythm into Pickle's existing visual identity. Do not copy the reference's fonts, colors, botanical motifs, commerce modules, or wording.

## Enhanced implementation prompt

Act as the creative director of a premium consumer-brand operating studio and as a senior frontend developer.

Improve the existing Pickle Advisors homepage, a founder-led advisory business for growing consumer brands. The site must explain three independent but connected businesses:

1. Pickle Advisors installs practical, human-controlled AI workflows around retailer orders, inventory, logistics, reporting, launches, invoicing, and cash.
2. Deet's Eats publishes consumer-market intelligence across Instagram, Unpackaged Goods, and The Deeter Digest.
3. Pickle VC is a clearly labeled Coming Soon vehicle that may eventually act on the combined operating and market intelligence.

The visual direction should feel **warm, editorial, handcrafted, commercially precise, and premium without becoming over-polished**.

Preserve Pickle's approved palette and identity:

- paper `#E8DED0`
- warm surface `#FFFDF7`
- soft cork `#D7CABC`
- ink `#10110F`
- editorial green `#087B36`
- deep green `#075C2A`
- lime `#B8FF38`
- strong editorial rules
- Georgia display moments, Archivo body/navigation, Space Grotesk metadata

Use the Organic Editorial template's design grammar in a Pickle-specific way:

- Let sections breathe instead of minimizing every gap.
- Create clear narrative chapters with varied density and layout.
- Use one deep-green operational section, warm paper media sections, a controlled ink-black investment-memo chapter for Pickle VC, and a warm-white founder close.
- Present Deet's Eats as a permanent four-source editorial directory. Do not make the homepage freshness depend on issue-specific artwork.
- Use a restrained editorial pull quote and one organic image mask in the founder section.
- Use overlaps or offsets only when they improve movement between sections.
- Keep buttons, labels, and legal boundaries precise and structured.
- Use subtle section transitions rather than abrupt black/cream alternation.

Required homepage flow:

1. Story-led buyer hero with concrete CPG work, AI Audit CTA, and compact trust proof.
2. Deep-green Pickle Advisors chapter with three concrete operational workflows and human-review boundary.
3. Warm editorial Deet's Eats chapter with equal-weight links to Instagram, TikTok, Unpackaged Goods, and The Deeter Digest.
4. Compact partnership line that preserves editorial independence.
5. Compact ink-black Pickle VC chapter with one thesis, three mandate facts, Coming Soon status, and legal boundary.
6. Founder note with real photo, concise biography, editorial pull quote, and one closing chapter that ties Build, Cover, and Back together before the final AI Audit CTA.

Copy must remain human, specific, and commercially literate. Name workflows, handoffs, and decisions. Avoid AI hype, generic transformation language, empty luxury language, and invented claims.

Do not add:

- botanical icons or generic leaf decoration
- beige wellness-brand clichés
- fake sustainability claims
- fake dashboards or metrics
- glassmorphism, gradients, blobs, or shadows
- sterile white card walls
- public advisory pricing
- testimonials or client logos without approval
- any suggestion that advisory buys media coverage or capital

Responsive acceptance:

- Desktop, 430px, and 390px must feel intentionally composed.
- The hero CTA must remain visible without excessive scrolling.
- Mobile must preserve breathing room without becoming a 10,000px stack of repeated giant headings.
- No horizontal overflow.
- Spotify and Substack must remain usable.
- AI Audit remains the only primary revenue CTA.
- The final candidate must flow better than the compact revision while remaining materially clearer than the rejected oversized candidate.

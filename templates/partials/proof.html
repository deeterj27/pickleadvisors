    The order is only part of the story.

    • Warehouse: 1,500 cases on hand
    • Other orders: 500 cases reserved
  2. 02 / AI reconciles the inputs

    Find the gap before you promise.

    Match the SKU, apply the 12-unit case pack, and subtract stock already promised to other customers.

    Order requires
    1,200 cases
    Available to promise
    1,000 cases

    The next 200 cases are expected Wednesday—after the requested delivery.

  3. 03 / Review a prepared response

    A shipment plan, ready to review.

    Prepare a split-shipment proposal, a retailer email, and warehouse instructions for the operator to review together.

    Review the proposed handoff

    Proposal: 1,000 cases for Tuesday; the remaining 200 after replenishment is confirmed.

    Approval: the operator verifies stock and transit timing, gets the retailer’s agreement, and approves the warehouse release. No email or shipment is sent automatically.

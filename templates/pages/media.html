Editorial standards

Independent judgment, explicit disclosure.

Deet's Eats distinguishes editorial coverage from paid work. Sponsored placements are clearly labeled. Brand partners may review factual accuracy, but editorial judgment remains independent.

Reporting links to primary sources where available and distinguishes announced agreements from completed transactions.

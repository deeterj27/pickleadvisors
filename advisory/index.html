Illustrative workflow

From scattered records
to a clearer month-end.

AI assists with repetitive preparation. Human review checks the work, resolves exceptions, and keeps decisions accountable.

  1. 01 / Gather + organize

    Start with the source.

    Bring together sales, bank activity, supplier invoices, and inventory records from the systems you already use.

    • Shopify, Amazon, Faire, or retailer sales
    • Bank records and supplier invoices
    • Inventory and freight costs
  2. 02 / Reconcile + review

    Find what does not agree.

    AI can help organize documents and suggest matches. A human reviews reconciliations, checks classifications, and resolves missing or inconsistent records.

    A mismatch is a question to resolve, not a number to guess.

  3. 03 / Report + decide

    See what needs attention.

    Bring the reviewed books into reporting on cash, outstanding balances, and the costs behind margin.

    Review the proposed handoff

    Output: a month-end reporting pack, open-items list, and questions for the next finance conversation.

    Approval: people review the numbers and decide what to do next. No payment or financial commitment is made automatically.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study or a measured result. Exact deliverables depend on agreed scope and available data.
